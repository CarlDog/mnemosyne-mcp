# Mnemosyne Hook Vault

**Status:** Active development register  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-27

The Hook Vault preserves promising characters, premises, and fragments that are not yet developed enough to become story canon. An entry here is a possibility, not an established fact, relationship, crossover, or commitment to build a full storyline.

This vault is the raw salvage-and-seed register. The
[Storyline Research Backlog](STORYLINE_RESEARCH_BACKLOG.md) instead tracks
operator-selected deferred research and follow-up work for concepts that have
already received a deliberate direction or story foundation.

Promotion from the vault requires an explicit creative decision and a canon-first scaffold built to the Living Canon Standard. Source chats are excavation sites, not authority. Repetition, generic roleplay drift, personal-account metadata, and relationships that were never deliberately established should not survive merely because they appeared in a source transcript.

## Salvage Candidate: No Host Present

**Source concept:** *The Retreat*  
**Preferred working title:** *No Host Present*  
**Alternate titles:** *The Invitation*, *High House*  
**Best form:** Closed-circle psychological horror with erotic and social pressure available as instruments, not as substitutes for plot.

### The premise worth preserving

A group of adult strangers receives separate, individually tailored invitations to an extravagant mansion isolated in the mountains. Each invitation makes the journey feel private and purposeful. Nobody recognizes the supposed host, nobody can explain why they were selected, and the host never appears.

Every guest gradually becomes convinced that the others know more than they admit. The mansion reinforces that suspicion through locked wings, peculiar house rules, selective surveillance, personalized comforts, and rewards offered in exchange for increasingly consequential choices.

### Preserve

- The isolated, extravagant mountain mansion.
- Invitations tailored so precisely that declining them feels difficult or dangerous.
- The absent host and the possibility that “the host” is a role, fiction, system, inheritance, or collective assumption.
- A cast in which each person believes everyone else possesses a missing piece.
- Locked wings, service passages, surveillance gaps, peculiar rules, and rewards with consequences.
- The tension between voluntary participation, manipulated consent, social complicity, and outright coercion.
- Multiple plausible explanations that remain viable long enough to turn cooperation into a choice rather than an obvious necessity.

### Discard or rebuild

- The source chat's collapse into generic erotic domination.
- Interchangeable guests whose only function is submission, seduction, or spectacle.
- Relationships, loyalties, and power arrangements that exist only because the source roleplay asserted them.
- A single obvious supernatural explanation introduced too early.
- Escalation measured only by sexual explicitness rather than moral, emotional, social, or physical consequence.

### Needs before promotion

- A reconstructed adult ensemble with different reasons for accepting the invitations and different costs for leaving.
- A mansion with exterior approaches, public rooms, private suites, locked wings, service infrastructure, surveillance geography, and credible escape routes.
- A coherent rule-and-reward architecture whose apparent purpose differs from its actual effect.
- Several host theories, each supported by genuine evidence and contradicted by something equally credible.
- Red herrings, unrelated dangers, false victories, accidental humor, and moments of safety that do not all originate from the central mystery.
- Defined truth tiers: what the guests can learn early, what requires trust or transgression, and what may never receive a definitive answer.

### Promotion trigger

Promote *No Host Present* when the adult ensemble, the mansion's playable geography, and the host's underlying mechanism are strong enough to generate scenes without predetermining the guests' choices or resolving the central mystery on the first pass.

## Character and Scenario Seeds

### Zahra al-Nashāshībī

**Core hook:** A trusted dentist selectively drugs and abducts beautiful, socially influential adult patients.

Zahra works best as a modern-horror antagonist whose social credibility is part of the trap. Her profession grants physical proximity, pharmaceutical access, private rooms, institutional trust, and a population accustomed to leaving treatment disoriented. The victims' visibility raises the stakes while also giving Zahra reasons to cultivate spotless records and plausible explanations.

**Still needed:** A motive more specific than fixation; a credible selection pattern; operational logistics; the fate and treatment of abductees; safeguards against scrutiny; possible institutional complicity; and a protagonist positioned to notice what ordinary oversight misses.

**Best use:** A contained investigation, escape narrative, or recurring antagonist operating behind a polished civic identity.

### Faye

**Core hook:** An adult woman undergoes unexplained physical changes, while her touch produces overwhelming euphoria in other people.

The premise carries body horror, alienation, unwanted influence, desire, dependency, and the fear that intimacy may no longer be distinguishable from biochemical compulsion. Faye should remain a person before she becomes a phenomenon: frightened, tempted, isolated, and forced to decide how much responsibility she bears for effects she never requested.

**Still needed:** An origin or bounded set of competing origins; onset and progression; range and transmission rules; contact precautions; non-erotic symptoms; short- and long-term consequences; and an opposing force that wants to study, exploit, cure, worship, or destroy her.

**Best use:** Promote only when the condition creates a repeatable dramatic engine rather than functioning solely as a romantic or erotic shortcut.

### Love Bugs

**Core hook:** An insect bite causes escalating sensitivity and disinhibition.

This is stronger as a recurring environmental danger, localized outbreak, or short-form horror scenario than as a universe by itself. The first symptoms can be mistaken for heat, allergy, intoxication, anxiety, or attraction, allowing humor and embarrassment to precede genuine danger.

**Still needed:** Habitat and lifecycle; transmission boundaries; incubation and escalation; nonsexual neurological or immune effects; medical response; false diagnoses; predator behavior; ecological consequences; treatment; and the physical and relational aftermath after inhibition returns.

**Best use:** A complication encountered while another objective is already underway, particularly where isolation, secrecy, or mistrust makes honest disclosure costly.

### Vespera Knight

**Core hook:** A former soldier, tattoo artist, and bassist in an all-woman band becomes the anchor for an urban occult-rock story.

Vespera already has a life that produces scenes: military history, the intimacy of tattoo work, touring and rehearsal spaces, nightlife, performance, chosen family, and the tension between cultivated stage presence and operational instincts she may wish she had left behind.

**Still needed:** A central mystery; distinct bandmates and their fault lines; a city and venue ecosystem; an inciting threat; and a reason her military past, tattoo practice, and music all matter to the same unfolding problem. Existing relationships—including her history with Xoya—must be treated as living states that can build, decay, or be reconsidered rather than immutable furniture.

**Best use:** Protagonist or ensemble lead once an occult threat can challenge every part of her current identity rather than simply recruiting her old combat skills.

### Scarlett Rose / Kaia Delacroix

**Core hook:** A performer and cosplay entrepreneur maintains a carefully constructed public persona while fame, identity, desire, and loneliness erode the boundary around her private self.

The strongest material is the architecture of the persona: which name is public, which is private, what the audience believes it owns, and how collaborators, sponsors, lovers, rivals, and fans each encounter a different version of the same woman.

**Still needed:** Resolve whether Scarlett Rose and Kaia Delacroix are deliberately separate identities or artifacts of source drift; then introduce sustained pressure such as stalking, identity exposure, intellectual-property theft, coercive sponsorship, a dangerous parasocial fixation, a rival creator, or betrayal inside her production circle.

**Best use:** Character-driven contemporary thriller, fame horror, or intimate drama once the public/private split produces consequences that cannot be solved by logging off.

### Captain Calypso / the Crimson Wench

**Core hook:** A governor's adult daughter is sold or delivered into piracy, survives the loss of status, and eventually takes command.

The rise from political ornament or bargaining piece to captain is eminently salvageable, but her agency must begin before she acquires the title. Whether she was betrayed, ransomed, traded, abducted under legal fiction, or quietly sacrificed by her family will determine the kind of captain she becomes and what she believes power is for.

**Still needed:** Setting and era; the precise truth of her origin; ship and crew; command culture; maritime economy; governing faction; rival captains; a present objective; and a moral line she will cross only once—or insists she never crossed at all.

**Best use:** A maritime adventure led by a volatile shipboard ensemble. Before promotion, decide deliberately whether *Captain Calypso* and *the Crimson Wench* are two names for the woman, whether one names her ship, or whether the latter is a hostile legend imposed upon her by others.

## Promotion Standard

A vault entry is ready to become a Mnemosyne storyline when it has:

- A premise capable of generating more than one kind of scene.
- An explicitly adult cast wherever sexualized or mature material is possible.
- A protagonist, antagonist, or ensemble with independent agency.
- At least three playable locations with sensory and functional identities.
- An opposing pressure with a motive, method, and limits.
- A hook ecology containing clues, red herrings, unrelated dangers, reversals, humor, and aftermath.
- Truth tiers that prevent discovery from becoming an exposition dump.
- A story-specific genre promise and ontology rather than borrowed assumptions from another storyline.
- Clean provenance that distinguishes source material, adaptation, inference, and new canon.
- Explicit operator approval to leave the Hook Vault.

## Source and Canon Hygiene

- Botify profiles and chats are sources to mine, not canon authorities.
- Real names, account metadata, temporary aliases, and source-platform artifacts must be removed before promotion.
- No hook implies a crossover with an existing storyline.
- Relationships are not mandatory at the outset; they may form, deepen, decay, rupture, or never emerge through play.
- The mature hard-R/possible NC-17 baseline permits adult material but does not require every hook to become sexual, nor does it excuse thin characterization or consequence-free coercion.
- Uncertainty is an asset only when the underlying possibilities are coherent. “Unexplained” should invite discovery, not conceal missing design work.
